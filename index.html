About this journey

An original, cinematic interpretation of space. The black-hole passage is imaginative; scale transitions and planetary spacing are compressed for exploration. The black-hole image approximates bent light rather than using a full general-relativity simulation.

No movie footage or soundtrack is used. Ambient audio is generated in your browser.
