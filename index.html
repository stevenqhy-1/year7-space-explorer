    PUT INFINITY IN PERSPECTIVE

    How big is big?

    Line up 105-metre soccer pitches end to end. About 86 billion would reach across Neptune’s orbit.

    Here, one “Solar System width” means 60 astronomical units: the approximate diameter of Neptune’s orbit. The Solar System extends much farther into the Oort Cloud.

    A light-year is the distance light travels in one year: about 9.46 trillion kilometres. A 50-light-year neighbourhood spans about 53,000 of these Solar System widths. A 100,000-light-year Milky Way spans 2,000 such neighbourhoods.

    The galaxies and cosmic-web comparisons use imagined regions of 10 million and 1 billion light-years. They are scale references, not measurements of the rendered scenes. All views are artistic, with compressed distances and enlarged objects.
