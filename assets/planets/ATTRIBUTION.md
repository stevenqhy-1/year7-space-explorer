Source files: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_NAME.jpg, where NAME is mercury, venus_atmosphere, earth_daymap, earth_clouds, mars, jupiter, saturn, uranus, neptune or moon. Stored here without image modification (Mars was downloaded as `2k_mars.jpg` and renamed `mars.jpg`; the others follow the same pattern).

The website adapts their appearance through lighting, small cloud/band movement, surface shading and atmospheric effects. Maps combine observed and reconstructed regions, with enhanced colours. They are not presented as untouched telescope photographs. Attribution is also available in the website's About this journey section.
